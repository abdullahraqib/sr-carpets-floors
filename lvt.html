What is LVT flooring?

LVT stands for luxury vinyl tile. It's a layered floor with a printed design and a clear, hard-wearing wear layer on top, made to look like real wood or stone at a fraction of the upkeep.

Because it copes with water and everyday spills, LVT is one of the most popular choices for kitchens and bathrooms. It's also comfortable and quiet underfoot, and a damp mop is all it needs to stay looking new.

Looks and formats

  • Wood-effect planksOak, walnut and weathered timber looks in a range of plank widths.
  • Stone and concrete tilesSlate, limestone, marble and polished concrete looks.
  • Herringbone and chevronClassic patterns without the cost or upkeep of real wood.
  • Borders and design stripsContrasting borders and strips to give a room a bespoke finish.

What's the difference between LVT and laminate?

Both can look like real wood. LVT is a vinyl floor, so it handles water better and feels softer and quieter underfoot. Laminate has a wood-fibre core and a very tough surface, which makes it great value for living areas and hallways.

Can LVT go in a bathroom or kitchen?

Yes. LVT is water-resistant and easy to clean, which is why it's one of the most popular floors for kitchens and bathrooms.

Does LVT work with underfloor heating?

Most LVT ranges are suitable for underfloor heating. We'll check the right range for your system when we measure.

What is a wear layer?

The wear layer is the clear protective top layer of LVT. A thicker wear layer stands up better to heavy traffic, so we'll suggest the right one for each room.
