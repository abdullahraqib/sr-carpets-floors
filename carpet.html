Carpet for every room in the house

Carpet is still the warmest, quietest floor you can put underfoot. It softens footsteps on stairs and landings, holds warmth in bedrooms and living rooms, and comes in more colours and textures than any other floor.

Our central Bradford showroom holds hundreds of rolls, so you can see and feel the real thing rather than a small sample. Once we've measured your home, we'll help you choose the right pile, fibre and underlay for how each room is used.

Which carpet is best for stairs?

Stairs take the heaviest wear in most homes, so a dense twist or a loop pile in a wool-rich or hard-wearing synthetic fibre is usually the best choice. We'll show you the options that suit your stairs in the showroom.

Is wool or synthetic carpet better?

Wool is naturally springy, warm and long-lasting, and feels luxurious underfoot. Synthetics such as polypropylene are very stain-resistant and good value, which suits busy family homes and homes with pets. Wool-rich blends sit between the two.

Do you supply underlay?

Yes. The right underlay makes a carpet feel better and last longer, and it's included in your written quotation.

How does the free measure work?

We visit your home, measure every room and check the subfloor, free of charge and with no obligation. Then you choose your carpet in our showroom and we give you a written quotation.
