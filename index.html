Our service

From first swatch to final threshold

Serving Bradford, Leeds and West Yorkshire for 50 years. We visit your home with samples, measure up, and fit the floor ourselves.

i.

Home consultation

We arrive with samples chosen around your brief and see them in your own light, against your own walls.

ii.

Survey & measure

Every room laser-measured and the subfloor checked for moisture and level, so the plan accounts for what lies beneath.

iii.

Written quotation

Flooring, underlay, furniture moving, subfloor preparation and removal of your old floor, all set out in one written quote.

iv.

Fitting & aftercare

Doors trimmed, offcuts removed, rooms returned as we found them. We come back if anything needs attention.

Craftsmanship
